About Rex Systems Bangladesh

Founded in 2018, Rex Systems Bangladesh has grown from a small development team into a technology partner trusted by 20+ businesses across retail, healthcare, recruitment, and professional services. Over 8+ years and 50+ delivered projects, we've stayed focused on one thing: building software that holds up under real business use, not just demos.

Our 15+ person team works across the full stack — from point-of-sale and e-commerce to ERP and hospital information systems — so you get a single accountable partner instead of juggling multiple vendors.
