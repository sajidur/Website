About Rex Systems Bangladesh

A Dhaka-based technology company helping businesses build, secure and operate reliable software.

Who we are

Rex Systems Bangladesh began as a custom software company, building business systems such as point of sale, e-commerce, ERP and hospital information systems for clients in Bangladesh and abroad.

Running those systems taught us where projects fail: insecure code, fragile servers and manual releases. Today we focus on four connected areas that address exactly those problems: application security, infrastructure management, DevOps and AI.

We work as an extension of your team, with written scope, transparent pricing and plain-language reporting.
